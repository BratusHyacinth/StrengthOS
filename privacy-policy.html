Privacy Policy

StrengthOS  ·  Effective: June 22, 2026

Summary: StrengthOS collects no personal data. Your information is stored only on your device and, where you enable it, in your own private iCloud account. The developer operates no server and never receives, accesses, sells, or shares your data.

They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition, and must not be relied upon as medical advice. Always consult a qualified physician or other healthcare professional before beginning any new exercise program or making any change to your diet, and never disregard or delay professional medical advice because of anything provided by the App.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the StrengthOS mobile application (the "App"), an independent iOS application provided by Michael Kolovos (the "Developer"). By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described herein. This policy does not apply to Apple Inc. or to any third-party services or platforms (including Apple iCloud and Apple Health), which are governed by their own respective terms and privacy policies.

2. No Collection of Personal Data

The Developer does not collect, receive, transmit, store, sell, rent, trade, or otherwise process any personal data through the App. The App contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting software, requests no advertising identifiers, and makes no network requests to any server controlled by the Developer. The App requires no account, login, or registration.

3. On-Device Storage

Data you create or that the App reads from Apple Health is stored locally on your device, protected by the operating system's standard on-device encryption. Data read from or derived from Apple Health (HealthKit), including, where authorized, heart rate, energy expenditure, workouts, sleep, dietary nutrition, and body composition (body mass, body fat percentage, lean body mass), together with any values computed from them, is retained solely on your device, is excluded from device backups, and is never written to iCloud, in accordance with Apple App Review Guideline 5.1.3. Apple Health access is optional, read access and write access may each be granted or revoked independently, and the App remains functional without it.

The App uses the device camera solely to read a printed nutrition-facts label when you choose to scan one while logging a meal; this text recognition is performed entirely on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks, no photograph or image is captured, stored, or transmitted, and camera access may be granted or revoked at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → StrengthOS.

When you describe a meal in words, whether typed in the App or spoken to Siri, that description is interpreted entirely on your device, using Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework together with the App's bundled food database; the text is not transmitted to the Developer or to any third-party server, and the App provides no cloud-processing path for it. Where you use Siri, the speech recognition itself is performed by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy, and the App receives only the resulting text.

4. iCloud Storage

Where you enable iCloud, your training-related data (such as workouts, exercises, templates, recovery preferences, logged meals, stretches, body measurements, and your manually entered profile) is synchronized across your own Apple devices through your private Apple iCloud account, encrypted in transit and at rest. This data resides within your personal iCloud account and is accessible only to you. Apple Inc. acts as the storage provider and processes such data subject to Apple's privacy policy. The Developer has no access to your iCloud data. You may disable iCloud synchronization at any time, in which case data remains on-device only.

5. No Advertising or Sale of Data

The App displays no advertisements, uses no advertising or tracking networks, and contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Because the Developer collects no personal data, no personal data is, or ever will be, sold or shared for advertising or any other purpose.

6. Legal Compliance

The App is designed to comply with all applicable data-protection and privacy laws, including without limitation the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), the New York SHIELD Act, and Apple's App Store and HealthKit requirements. Because the Developer collects and processes no personal data, the rights afforded under such laws, including rights of access, correction, portability, deletion, and objection, are satisfied directly through your own control of the data on your device and in your iCloud account, as described in Section 8.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal data from any person. As the App collects no personal data from any user, no special category of risk to minors arises. Parents or guardians with questions or concerns may contact the Developer at mkolovos327@gmail.com.

8. Your Control and Data Deletion

You retain complete control over all data associated with the App at all times. To export or remove your data:

  • Data export: export a copy of your data, including Apple Health–derived data, as CSV or JSON files via Settings → Export Data within the App. Export files are created on your device and leave it only if you choose to share or save them elsewhere.
  • On-device data: delete the App from your device to remove all locally stored data, including all Apple Health–derived data.
  • iCloud data: delete the App's iCloud data via iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → StrengthOS. Signing out of iCloud does not delete data already stored in iCloud.
  • Apple Health access: revoke read or write permissions at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → StrengthOS.

Because the Developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing for the Developer to retrieve, export, or delete on your behalf. Deletion is permanent and cannot be restored by the Developer.
