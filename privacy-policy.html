Privacy Policy

StrengthOS  ·  Effective: June 18, 2026

In short: StrengthOS collects no personal data. Your information is stored only on your device and, where you enable it, in your own private iCloud account. The developer operates no server and never receives, accesses, sells, or shares your data.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the StrengthOS mobile application (the "App"), an independent iOS application provided by Michael Kolovos (the "Developer"). By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described herein. This policy does not apply to Apple Inc. or to any third-party services or platforms (including Apple iCloud and Apple Health), which are governed by their own respective terms and privacy policies.

2. No Collection of Personal Data

The Developer does not collect, receive, transmit, store, sell, rent, trade, or otherwise process any personal data through the App. The App contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting software, requests no advertising identifiers, and makes no network requests to any server controlled by the Developer. The App requires no account, login, or registration.

3. On-Device Storage

Data you create or that the App reads from Apple Health is stored locally on your device, protected by the operating system's standard on-device encryption. Data read from or derived from Apple Health (HealthKit) — including, where authorized, heart rate, energy expenditure, workouts, sleep, and dietary nutrition, together with any values computed from them — is retained solely on your device and is never written to iCloud, in accordance with Apple App Review Guideline 5.1.3. Apple Health access is optional, read access and write access may each be granted or revoked independently, and the App remains functional without it.

4. iCloud Storage

Where you enable iCloud, your training-related data (such as workouts, exercises, templates, recovery preferences, logged meals, stretches, body measurements, and your manually entered profile) is synchronized across your own Apple devices through your private Apple iCloud account, encrypted in transit and at rest. This data resides within your personal iCloud account and is accessible only to you. Apple Inc. acts as the storage provider and processes such data subject to Apple's privacy policy. The Developer has no access to your iCloud data. You may disable iCloud synchronization at any time, in which case data remains on-device only.

5. No Advertising or Sale of Data

The App displays no advertisements, uses no advertising or tracking networks, and contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Because the Developer collects no personal data, no personal data is, or ever will be, sold or shared for advertising or any other purpose.

6. Legal Compliance

The App is designed to comply with all applicable data-protection and privacy laws, including without limitation the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Apple's App Store and HealthKit requirements. Because the Developer collects and processes no personal data, the rights afforded under such laws — including rights of access, correction, portability, deletion, and objection — are satisfied directly through your own control of the data on your device and in your iCloud account, as described in Section 8.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal data from any person. As the App collects no personal data from any user, no special category of risk to minors arises.

8. Your Control and Data Deletion

You retain complete control over all data associated with the App at all times. To remove your data:

  • On-device data: delete the App from your device to remove all locally stored data, including all Apple Health–derived data.
  • iCloud data: remove the App's data via iOS Settings → your name → iCloud, or sign out of iCloud.
  • Apple Health access: revoke read or write permissions at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → StrengthOS.

Because the Developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing for the Developer to retrieve, export, or delete on your behalf.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any revised version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date. Should the App ever introduce functionality that collects personal data, this policy will be amended and the change disclosed before such functionality ships. Your continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
